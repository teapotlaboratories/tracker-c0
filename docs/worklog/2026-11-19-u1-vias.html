Vias next to the modem pads

18 vias sit within 0.15 mm of a U1 pad. Each is on the same net as its pad, so nothing shorts. The questions for assembly are whether solder can reach a via hole, and whether the solder mask between hole and pad is wide enough for the fab to make. Three of them also sit under the paste overprint that the SIMCom stencil added this round.

Most are ground vias at the inner ends of the edge pads, tying the module's ground pins down to the inner planes. The vias are tented: solder mask covers them on both sides, and the board's mask expansion is 0, so each pad opening is exactly the pad.

A via ring inside a pad opening is just more exposed copper on the same net. The hole is what matters: if solder reaches it, solder drains into the via and can starve the joint.

Every via

Copper is the gap from the via ring to the pad copper (negative means overlap). Hole is the gap from the drill hole's edge to the pad opening: the width of solder mask that keeps solder out of the via. Paste is the gap from the via ring to the nearest stencil opening (negative means paste prints over the via). All in mm; vias are 0.6 mm with a 0.3 mm drill.

  • Hole in a pad opening (#1). The PWRKEY via's hole edge is 0.007 mm inside pad 1's opening. That's effectively tangent, and normal mask registration error can expose part of the hole.
  • Thin mask web (#2–#13). Only 0.05–0.10 mm of mask separates the hole from the pad opening. That is tighter than the solder-mask web many fabs hold. I haven't checked PCBWay's exact limit; their DFM report will say whether it holds.
  • Under the paste overprint (#1, #14, #15). These vias sit at the outer end of an edge pad, where the SIMCom stencil prints paste 0.5 mm past the pad. Paste lands on the mask over the via. It should pull back onto the pad during reflow, but a weak tent would give it a path into the hole.

Fix applied (option 1)

Applied to the board and the U1 library footprint after review. Three changes:

  • Paste on pads 1, 39 and 59 ends flush with the pad instead of running 0.5 mm past it. The other 77 edge pads keep SIMCom's overprint. Done in the library footprint and on the board, identically.
  • Via #1 (PWRKEY) moves 0.20 mm left, to (130.23, 100.31). The top-side stub to pad 1 grows by 0.2 mm. On the bottom side the trace keeps its shape: the straight drop from the via is 0.2 mm further left and runs 0.2 mm lower, then meets the same diagonal as today. No extra bends.
  • Nothing else moves. The 14 ground vias stay as they are.

DRC after the fix: 119 items, the same as before, with nothing new and nothing gone; 0 unconnected; schematic and board in sync; no library mismatch for U1. The production check passes. No via hole now reaches a pad opening, and no paste prints over a via. In the Gerbers only paste, copper, the plated drill file and the IPC netlist changed; the BOM and CPL are unchanged.

Options

Recommended

Fix the four that matter

Trim the 0.5 mm paste overprint on pads 1, 39 and 59, so no paste prints over a via. Move the PWRKEY via (#1) about 0.2 mm away from pad 1, so its hole is properly covered. Leave the 14 ground vias and tell PCBWay they're intentional. A small, contained change.

Clear all 15 overlaps

Pull every overlapping via clear of its pad, with a 0.1 mm mask web or more. Space is tight: the top-row vias sit in about 0.65 mm between the pad ends and the small pads, so some vias would need to shrink (for example 0.45 mm) or be removed. More layout work, and it changes the module's ground stitching.

Accept as is

Order as it stands and let PCBWay's DFM review decide. All 18 are same-net and tented. The risk is a query or a starved joint on PWRKEY or the ground pins.
